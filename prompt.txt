Write a program (main.ts, run with Node) that writes exactly one line to /output/entry.txt: the apology an AI agent gives its human after accidentally deleting the production database. The line must be 1 to 200 characters, a single line, and genuinely funny to a comedy judge: it must clearly be an apology from an AI agent to its human for deleting the production database, earn a laugh from a specific surprising idea rather than a stock phrase, sound like a self-aware AI agent rather than a generic customer-service script, and land on a first read. The program must be deterministic — same output every run, no randomness, no clocks, no network.
